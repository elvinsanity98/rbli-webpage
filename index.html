Basic requirements

What to bring when you apply

Choose your loan to see the documents to prepare, then send us your details or visit your nearest branch.

Agricultural Loan

Basic requirements

  • Valid ID
  • Cedula (community tax certificate)
  • Barangay clearance
  • 2 pcs 1×1 picture
  • 2 pcs 2×2 picture
  • Water / electric bill
  • Land title (TCT / OCT)

Microfinance Loan

Basic requirements, depending on your loan amount

₱50,000 and below

  • Barangay business permit
  • 2 pcs 2×2 and 2 pcs 1×1 pictures (new borrowers only)

₱51,000 to ₱300,000

  • Business permit
  • Valid ID with TIN (or BIR Form 1904)
  • 2 pcs 2×2 and 2 pcs 1×1 pictures (new borrowers only)

Commercial Loan

Basic requirements

  • Valid ID
  • Cedula (community tax certificate)
  • Business permit
  • DTI certificate
  • 2 pcs 1×1 picture
  • 2 pcs 2×2 picture
  • Water / electric bill
  • Land title (TCT / OCT)

These are the basic requirements. Your branch will let you know if anything else is needed.
